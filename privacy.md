# Privacy Policy for Recolor for Firefox

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Recolor for Firefox ("the extension") does not collect, store, sell or share
any personal data or usage data. Nothing you do with the extension ever
leaves your device.

## What the extension stores

- **Your background image.** If you upload a custom background, it is
  stored in your browser using Firefox's local extension storage
  (`storage.local`). It is never uploaded anywhere. Before saving it, the
  extension re-encodes the image, which removes embedded metadata such as
  EXIF and location data.

Nothing else is stored.

## What the extension does on websites

To place your background behind websites and darken light pages, the
extension reads the colors and layout of the pages you visit and changes
how they look. This happens entirely inside your browser. The extension
does not read, record or send page content, browsing history, form
input, cookies or any other information.

## Permissions

- **storage**: saves your background image locally, as described above.
- **Access to websites**: lets the extension restyle the pages you visit.
  It is used only to change how pages look.

## What the extension does not do

- No analytics, tracking, telemetry or advertising.
- No accounts, sign-ins or remote servers.
- No network requests to the developer or any third party.
- No selling, sharing or transferring of data, because none is collected.

## Your control

To delete everything the extension has stored, remove the extension
from Firefox (about:addons). This deletes the saved background image.
Because no data is collected, there is nothing held anywhere else to
request, correct or delete.

## Children

The extension collects no information from anyone, including children
under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the
extension's repository with a new effective date. Any change that
introduces data collection would require your consent first.
